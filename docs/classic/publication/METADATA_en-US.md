## Description

Bring everyone into the game with Classic, a local party game for 3–20 players
on one phone. Pass the phone to see your role privately, then discuss and vote
in person. Reveal the result when your group is ready and play again.

Play unlimited free rounds with three included categories. Adjust the number of
impostors, turn on a category hint, add a discussion timer, or try Troll mode.
Gameplay works offline and does not require an account.

Optional Premium subscriptions unlock extra categories. Annual and weekly plans
provide the same Premium category access while the subscription is active.
Prices and billing periods are displayed in the app before purchase. Manage
subscriptions through your Apple account; Restore Purchases is available in the app.
Extra categories may be available only in English. Interface languages include
English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic.
