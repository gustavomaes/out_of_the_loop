# App Review notes — draft to complete against the signed candidate

Status: blocked by STAGE_8 external gates. Do not paste unfinished notes into a submission.

This update replaces the previous Guided experience with Classic. It is played
locally by 3–20 people using one phone. There is no login. Discussion and voting
happen in person; the app distributes roles and reveals the result after confirmation.

Free review path: launch → configure at least three players → Start → privately
reveal and cover each role → Next → discussion → confirm reveal → result → Play again.
The three free categories and unlimited rounds do not require a subscription.

Subscription review path: open Premium from home, a locked category, Settings,
or result. Annual and weekly subscriptions unlock the same additional categories.
Prices/periods come from the store. Category selection still requires confirmation
after a contextual purchase. Settings provides restore and subscription management.
A cancelled or pending purchase does not grant access. Expiry affects the next
round, without interrupting an existing round. Legacy rights use an audited mapping.
