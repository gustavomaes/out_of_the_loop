## Prompt-base

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one premium high fidelity mobile app visual concept for an original US English imposter word party game, provisional brand 'Oddly'. Output a polished portrait presentation with ONE large straight-on iPhone screen, crisp and readable, nearly fills the image, small concept number/title outside the phone at top. Not a collage. The screen is the initial game setup, light minimalist UI with vibrant accents for young adults meeting friends, family-safe. Native feasible Flutter design, generous whitespace but all essential controls above fold. iPhone status bar at top. Brand Oddly, help and settings icons. Short heading 'Who’s bluffing?' then subtitle 'One phone. Your whole crew.' Segmented mode selector 'Quick' selected and 'Guided' unselected. A player count control with minus, '6 players', plus; small optional 'Edit names' link. Categories row 'Categories' and 'Free mix' with chevron, subtitle '6 categories ready to play'. Options area compact showing 'Imposters' / 'Auto · 2' and row 'Hint off · Timer off' with 'Game options' chevron. Large primary 'Start game' button, small '3–20 players · Play offline' beneath, secondary 'How to play'. No online mode, no bottom nav, no compulsory paywall, no coins, no leaderboard, no secret role exposed. Own identity, no lime green competitor branding, no stock photography, no heavy illustration, no tiny illegible copy. All text in English inside app. Exact concept title supplied below outside phone. Use polished realistic UI typography and consistent spacing. Different layout and component composition appropriate to this direction, not merely a recolor.

## 01 AZUL ESSENCIAL

Calm precision and effortless setup. Cool off-white background #F7F8FC, cobalt blue #3154D5, midnight navy text. Crisp geometric sans font, restrained medium rounded corners 16px. Main players control large horizontal white card with subtle gray border and clean outlined stepper buttons. Categories and options in compact divided list below. Bold full-width cobalt CTA with white text. Tiny original overlapping speech-card logo. Sophisticated airy Apple-like clarity, no gradients. Concept heading outside phone: '01 · AZUL ESSENCIAL'.

## 02 CORAL SOCIAL

Warm welcoming social energy. Warm ivory background #FFF9F3, rich burnt coral #C84232 CTA with white text, ink brown text, peach and subtle sunny yellow accents. Friendly rounded sans, soft pill buttons, thin hand-drawn-looking but professional tiny speech mark brand. Players section is open spacious composition with a row of six minimal circular numbered avatars above a large count and capsule stepper, edit names small. Categories and options are soft cream compact cards. Minimal and mature, no children cartoon and no busy decorative objects. Concept heading outside phone: '02 · CORAL SOCIAL'.

## 03 ROXO LÚDICO

Playful but refined contemporary game utility. Pure white/light lavender #F7F5FF background, deep violet #6840C9 actions with white text, tiny peach accents, near-black text. Rounded geometric typography with strong hierarchy. Graphic brand symbol two offset rounded cards with one missing corner, small. Main configuration organized as one large softly rounded light lavender panel with a prominent player stepper at top and neatly divided category/option rows within. Segmented mode pill above panel. Large violet CTA outside panel. Subtle flat card motif integrated at header only; UI not illustration-dominated. Concept heading outside phone: '03 · ROXO LÚDICO'.

## 04 EDITORIAL POP

Bold editorial minimalism with own personality, warm white #FFFEF8 background, black typography, saturated golden yellow #FFD447 primary button with black text, tiny electric blue secondary accents. Oversized condensed bold headline with normal readable humanist sans body. Fine black 1px borders, 8px corners, flat no chunky shadows. Player count is oversized typographic '6' between square outlined minus/plus buttons, small 'PLAYERS' label. Categories and options arranged as structured editorial rows with dividing hairlines and small uppercase labels. Small offset word-card brand icon. Clean functional mature playful visual; not dense neo-brutalism. Concept heading outside phone: '04 · EDITORIAL POP'.
